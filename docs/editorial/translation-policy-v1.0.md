# Translation & Editorial Policy v1.0

**Status:** proposed policy, not an approved or completed translation.

## Editorial objective
Publish a **faithful, complete, independently reviewable** Traditional Chinese original and English translation of two works by 江慶淇. This is distinct from the selective practical guide Living Awakening／覺醒生活.

## Rules
1. Original Traditional Chinese is authoritative. Preserve wording, order, section breaks, quoted material, names, dates, numbers and attribution; never silently abridge or modernize.
2. Record textual corrections (e.g. PDF line joins, misrecognized Chinese characters) in a correction ledger, with PDF page/printed page, original extraction, corrected text, reviewer and evidence.
3. Translate paragraphs faithfully with consistent tone and terminology. Do not supplement or soften doctrine, metaphysical claims, health claims or scientific assertions. Preserve clear separation of **author's belief/argument** from externally established empirical claims.
4. Do not invent or embellish quotations or bibliographic sources. Mark unresolved text/translation `needs-review`; such entries must not become production content.
5. Use book/chapter/paragraph stable IDs for cross-language alignment. Record any necessary split/merge with explicit crosswalk rather than resetting IDs.
6. Keep publication year, edition, printed pagination and source PDF pagination distinct. The original book's chapters and prefaces remain in original editorial order.
7. If original contains copyrighted quoted text or third-party images, explicitly assess reuse rights before online publication/translation.
8. Reader-facing English translation should be labeled as translation (not the author's original English text) and include translator/reviewer credit where agreed.
